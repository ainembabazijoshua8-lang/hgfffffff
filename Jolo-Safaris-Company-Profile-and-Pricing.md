# Jebri Safaris Company Profile & Pricing Guide

Prepared from the current website content and tour/activity catalog.

## 1. Company Overview

Jebri Safaris is an East African travel company built around personal, transparent, and conservation-led safari experiences. The company is rooted in Tanzania and offers curated adventures across Tanzania and Kenya, with a strong focus on wildlife safaris, mountain treks, cultural immersion, Zanzibar beach escapes, and premium add-on experiences.

Jebri Safaris positions itself as the kind of operator travelers wish they could book with from the start: small enough to care personally, experienced enough to manage every detail, and committed to authenticity rather than mass-market processing.

### Brand Promise
- Personal, thoughtful safari planning
- Local expertise and on-the-ground knowledge
- Conservation-minded travel
- Respectful community partnerships
- Clear, transparent pricing

### Founded
- Established: 2026
- Base: Arusha, Tanzania

### Founders

- Lowasa Gibson, Co-Founder

## 2. What Jebri Safaris Offers

### Core Travel Experiences
- Wildlife safaris in the Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Tarangire, Ruaha, Selous, Lake Manyara, and Maasai Mara
- Kilimanjaro trekking and premium summit climbs
- Cultural tours with Maasai, Hadzabe, Datoga, and coffee farm experiences
- Zanzibar beach and culture trips
- Tailored luxury private safaris and honeymoon escapes
- Add-on activities such as balloon flights, dhow cruises, snorkeling, spa treatments, and more

### Signature Travel Themes
- Great Migration journeys
- Big-cat and predator-focused safaris
- Family-friendly safari adventures
- Photography-focused safaris
- Birding specialist trips
- Private luxury expeditions
- Wellness and beach retreats

## 3. Company Values

Jebri Safaris is guided by the following values:

- Conservation first: protecting wildlife and supporting conservation work
- Community-led: backing local communities and cultural experiences
- Craft over scale: small-group, thoughtful travel rather than large-volume tourism
- Local expertise: Tanzanian-born guides and operators with deep regional knowledge
- Transparency: clear communication, honest pricing, and dependable service

## 4. Why Travelers Choose Jebri Safaris

- Local guides who understand the land, wildlife, and road conditions
- Strong emphasis on meaningful rather than rushed travel
- Flexible itinerary planning with custom quotes
- Premium and budget-friendly options in one company
- Strong mix of classic safari experiences and unusual, high-impact adventures
- Support for community projects and anti-poaching efforts
